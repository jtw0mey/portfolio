    US Telehealth Usage

    US Telehealth Usage Analysis

    Tools: SQL (Google BigQuery) | Tableau  |  Data Sources: US Government Dataset | US Census Bureau

    Project Overview

    Telehealth allows patients to access healthcare remotely using digital technology. During the COVID-19 pandemic, telehealth evolved from a niche option into a critical healthcare delivery channel.

    In this project, I analyzed US government telehealth data to answer three core questions:

    • Where is telehealth being used the most across the US?
    • How fast did adoption grow between 2019 and 2022?
    • Which types of remote care are patients using most often?

    1. Analyzing Total Usage by State

    To find out which states had the highest overall volume of telehealth interactions, I uploaded the dataset to Google BigQuery and ran an aggregation query grouping total service uses by state.







    While California led in total volume with over 48 million uses, total counts can be misleading due to population differences across states. To measure true adoption rates, I imported US Census population data into SQL and calculated per-capita usage (total services divided by population).








    Sorting by per-capita usage reveals a completely different picture. California drops to 15th place in overall engagement, while Massachusetts (2.37 uses per person) and Arizona (2.06 uses per person) emerge as the clear leaders in actual adoption.







    2. Tracking YoY Growth (2018–2022)

    Next, I analyzed annual service volumes in BigQuery to evaluate how adoption shifted leading up to and during the COVID-19 pandemic.



    Telehealth usage experienced a massive 26x surge between 2019 (~3.8M uses) and 2020 (~102.7M uses). This sudden acceleration aligns directly with pandemic-era lockdown mandates and expanded public healthcare coverage for remote services. Usage peaked in 2021 before beginning to stabilize in 2022.







    3. Utilization by Service Type

    To understand how patients were receiving care, I queried the aggregate usage broken down by telehealth delivery method.



    Live Audio/Video calls were the overwhelming choice, driving over 292M total interactions. Other options—such as Store and Forward (~2.1M uses) and Remote Patient Monitoring (~983k uses)—represented only a small fraction of overall usage.








    To clarify the exact market share of each delivery method, I updated the query using a window function to calculate each modality’s percentage of total volume.



    Live Audio/Video made up 98.5% of all telehealth engagements. Secondary methods like Store and Forward (0.7%) and Remote Patient Monitoring (0.3%) accounted for less than 1% each.

    4. Interactive Data Visualizations

    To present these insights, I built two interactive Tableau dashboards:

    • Dashboard 1: Executive Overview — Synthesizes long-term monthly volume trends, geographic density maps, and service breakdowns to provide a complete view of adoption from 2018 to 2022.
    • Dashboard 2: 2022 Year-Over-Year Performance — Tracks post-peak stabilization by comparing 2022 metrics against 2021. Highlights key changes, including a 26% decline in live video visits alongside growth in specialized services like Store & Forward (+90%) and Remote Patient Monitoring (+42%).
